轻卷隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月15日
开发者：地瓜猫工作室
联系邮箱：sweet-potato-cat@hotmail.com

屏幕捕获与本地处理

轻卷在你通过系统界面明确授权后接收屏幕画面，用于拼接长截图。所有图像处理和文字识别在 iPhone 本地进行。轻卷没有账号、广告、分析或跟踪 SDK，不提供素材上传或云端处理服务，不访问相册、麦克风或摄像头。你可随时通过系统录屏控件停止捕获。

本地保存与删除

截图、源录屏及各张截图的编辑历史保存在受系统保护的应用／App Group 容器中，并排除系统备份。复制并删除会在独立 PNG 数据交付本机剪贴板后删除对应截图；分享并删除仅在系统报告分享成功后删除，取消或失败会保留。其他截图继续保留，删除最后一张时清理整个任务和共享源录屏。导出 PNG／PDF 保留草稿；也可使用“丢弃草稿”主动清理。清理不经过相册，不会进入“最近删除”。异常退出时保留已完成片段和已提交编辑供恢复。

分享与遮挡

复制仅限本机剪贴板。你主动通过系统面板分享或保存成品时，所选接收服务按其自身隐私政策处理文件。马赛克及兼容的旧实色遮挡会烧录进导出图像，PDF 不包含隐藏的未遮挡原图或 OCR 文本层。马赛克不保证敏感内容无法被推测，自动识别也可能遗漏；请在分享前复核。

通知与诊断

完成通知是可选的，拒绝授权不影响截取。通知仅含本地任务／录制标识，不含屏幕内容。本地诊断记录状态码、计时和计数，不含画面、识别文字、应用名称或文件路径，不自动上传，随任务删除。开发验收可在用户主动操作下导出原素材和诊断到本机缓存；普通使用不会自动导出或发送。联系开发者时请勿主动发送敏感截图；你自行发送的邮件及附件由邮箱服务商处理，并仅用于答复及排查该反馈，可联系上方邮箱申请删除开发者持有的反馈材料。

自愿赞赏

所有功能均可免费使用。赞赏通过 Apple App 内购买处理，是可重复的一次性付款，不是订阅，不解锁功能。Apple 处理付款和退款；轻卷不接收银行卡或支付账户资料，仅在设备上核验交易并完成赞赏处理，不将交易信息上传到开发者服务器，也不建立赞赏者档案。

联系与更新

如对本政策、本地数据清理或你主动提供的反馈材料有疑问，请联系 sweet-potato-cat@hotmail.com。本政策随功能变化更新；公开网页托管及邮件服务商可能依据其自身政策处理访问记录或通信信息，这不代表轻卷上传了截图或录屏。

Scrolloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026
Developer: Potato Cat Studio
Contact: sweet-potato-cat@hotmail.com

Screen capture and on-device processing

Scrolloom receives screen frames only after you explicitly authorize capture through the system interface. Images and text recognition are processed on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs, media uploads, or cloud processing. The app does not access Photos, the microphone, or the camera. You can stop capture at any time using the system recording control.

Local storage and deletion

Screenshots, source recordings, and independent edit histories are stored in protected app/App Group storage and excluded from system backup. Copy & Delete removes the relevant screenshot after delivering independent PNG data to the local clipboard. Share & Delete removes it only after the system reports successful sharing; cancellation or failure preserves it. Other screenshots remain. Deleting the last screenshot clears the task and shared source recordings. Export PNG/PDF keeps your draft; Discard draft lets you clear it yourself. Cleanup does not use Photos or its Recently Deleted album. Completed segments and committed edits remain recoverable after interruption.

Sharing and redactions

Copy is local to this device. When you choose to share or save an export through the system sheet, your chosen destination handles the file under its own privacy policy. Mosaic and compatible older solid redactions are flattened into exported pixels; PDF files contain no hidden unredacted image or OCR text layer. Mosaic does not guarantee that sensitive content cannot be inferred, and recognition may miss information. Review your export before sharing.

Notifications and diagnostics

Completion notifications are optional; denying permission does not prevent capture. They contain only local task/capture identifiers, not screen content. Local diagnostics record status codes, timings, and counters, without images, recognized text, app names, or file paths. They are not automatically uploaded and are deleted with the task. Developer acceptance testing can explicitly export source media and diagnostics to the device cache; ordinary use does not automatically export or send them. Do not send sensitive screenshots when requesting support. Email and attachments you choose to send are handled by your email provider and used only to answer and investigate your request. Contact the address above to request deletion of feedback materials held by the developer.

Optional tips

Every feature is free. Tips use Apple In-App Purchase and are repeatable, one-time payments, not subscriptions or feature unlocks. Apple handles payment and refunds. Scrolloom does not receive card or payment-account details. It verifies and finishes transactions on your device, without uploading transaction information to a developer server or maintaining supporter profiles.

Contact and updates

For questions about this policy, local data removal, or feedback you have sent, contact sweet-potato-cat@hotmail.com. This policy is updated as features change. The public website host and email providers may process access records or communications under their own policies; this does not mean Scrolloom uploads screenshots or recordings.
