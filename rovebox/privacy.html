RoveBox 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日。开发者：地瓜猫工作室。

本机文件和设置

RoveBox 在你通过系统选择器、打开文件或拖放等方式允许的范围内浏览、预览和处理文件。路径、收藏、工作区、偏好、自定义图标、任务恢复资料和智能整理学习记录保存在本机，不会发送到开发者服务器。应用不含广告、开发者运营的使用分析或跨应用跟踪。

智能整理

智能整理使用文件名和必要元数据，不读取正文。学习需要你主动启用，使用应用内成功的手动移动和明确反馈。整理建议需要你确认才会执行。你可关闭学习或清除记录。应用不会向大模型服务上传文件名、文件内容或学习记录。只有你明确请求时，应用才通过 Apple 的系统功能取得系统语义资源。

网页、云盘、服务器和共享

HTML 预览可能请求网页引用的图片、样式等网络资源，相关服务器可能收到这些请求及通常随附的信息。链接由默认浏览器打开。被动浏览、选中、预览和后台搜索不会主动下载云占位文件。你明确复制、移动、打开或下载时，macOS 或云盘提供者可能取得所需内容。云盘同步、挂载服务器认证由系统或对应服务管理。RoveBox 不在保存的服务器地址中存储密码。

打开、共享或在 Finder 中显示文件时，应用使用 macOS 提供的接口。安装和首次启动不自动改变文件关联。商店更新和 Apple 的可选诊断由 Apple 按其政策处理。

试用、购买和兑换

21 天试用、一次性购买、找回购买记录及官方兑换码由 Apple App Store 和 StoreKit 处理。应用不接收银行卡资料或支付密码，也无需注册 RoveBox 账号。应用在本机核验商品和交易标识、原始购买日期与撤销状态，判断试用及购买状态，并允许已有草稿和任务安全收尾。交易信息不发送给开发者。macOS 14 的兑换入口会打开 Apple 的兑换网页。购买与文件夹授权相互独立。

保留、删除与联系

清除文件任务历史不会删除实际文件，也不会清除智能整理学习记录。未完成任务保留恢复所需信息。卸载应用后，本机记录可能仍然保留。设置包只在你明确导出时创建，可能包含文件名、路径和学习记录。导入不会转移购买或文件访问权限，也不会运行旧任务。导入前的本机备份不自动上传。

请通过 sweet-potato-cat@hotmail.com 联系开发者。你可以自行提供脱敏日志或样例来帮助排查，应用不会自动发送这些内容。公开网页及邮件服务商可能按其政策处理访问信息或邮件。本政策会随功能或数据处理方式的变化更新。

RoveBox Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026. Developer: Potato Cat Studio.

Files and settings on your Mac

RoveBox browses, previews, and manages files within the access you grant through a system picker, opening files, or drag and drop. Paths, favorites, workspaces, preferences, custom icons, task recovery information, and Smart Organize learning records stay on your Mac and are not sent to developer servers. The app has no advertising, developer-operated usage analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Smart Organize

Smart Organize uses file names and necessary metadata without reading file contents. If you enable learning, it uses successful manual moves and explicit feedback in the app. You approve suggestions before they run. You can turn off learning or clear its records. File names, file contents, and learning records are not uploaded to large language model services. System language resources are obtained through macOS only when you explicitly request them.

Web pages, cloud storage, servers, and sharing

HTML previews may request referenced images, styles, and other online resources. Their servers may receive those requests and the information normally included with them. Web links open in your default browser. Passive browsing, selection, previewing, and background search do not actively download cloud placeholders. When you explicitly copy, move, open, or download a file, macOS or your provider may fetch its contents. The system or provider handles synchronization and mounted server authentication. Saved server addresses do not include passwords.

RoveBox uses macOS interfaces to open or share files and reveal them in Finder. Installation and first launch do not change file associations automatically. Apple handles App Store updates and optional Apple diagnostics under its own policies.

Trial, purchases, and redemption

The 21-day trial, one-time purchase, purchase restoration, and official offer codes are processed by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. RoveBox does not receive card or payment-password details and does not require a RoveBox account. The app verifies product and transaction identifiers, original purchase dates, and revocation information locally to determine access and allow existing drafts and tasks to finish safely. Transaction information is not sent to the developer. On macOS 14, redemption opens Apple's redemption page. Purchases do not grant access to folders.

Retention, deletion, and contact

Clearing task history does not delete your files or Smart Organize learning records. Unfinished tasks keep the information needed for recovery. Local records may remain after uninstalling. Settings packages are created only when you explicitly export them and may contain file names, paths, or learning records. Imports do not transfer purchases or folder permissions and do not run old tasks. Local backups kept before an import are not uploaded automatically.

Contact the developer at sweet-potato-cat@hotmail.com. You can choose to include redacted logs or samples in a support request. The app does not automatically send these materials. Website and email providers may handle access records or correspondence under their own policies. We update this policy when features or data handling change.
